# GRC205---Incident-Management-and-Business-Continuity
Business continuity and disaster recovery plan for a fictional flood and power outage at Northbridge Skills Institute: BIA, recovery objectives, backup design, strategy and budget, runbook, tabletop exercise, and KPIs.
This project is my submission for GRC205 (Incident Management and Business Continuity, Week 21) at the International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA). It responds to a fictional scenario in which flooding and a power failure close the main site of Northbridge Skills Institute, a training provider with 1,800 learners and 90 staff, for an estimated 72 hours, three hours before an examination.

What it covers

Business Impact Analysis: five functions (teaching and assessment, fees, admissions, payroll, records) with owners, dependencies, minimum service, priorities and worked disruption-cost calculations for 8 and 24 hours.
Recovery objectives and backups: RTO, RPO and MTD targets, checkpoint age analysis, and a backup design using the 3-2-1 principle with an independent immutable or offline copy.
Strategy and budget: a comparison of Cold, Warm and Hot recovery packages against a NGN 3,000,000 annual limit, with an itemised budget, a recommendation and residual risks.
Coordinated plan: activation thresholds, RACI, temporary procedures for a 25-workstation limit, a DR runbook, a communication matrix and a learner notice.
Recovery schedule and exercise: a two-technician schedule with the critical dependency path, a 45-minute tabletop with a decision log, and corrective actions.
Governance: testing calendar, mapping to NIST SP 800-34 and ISO 22301 themes, KPIs and a maturity assessment.

All organisations, systems and figures are fictional. The recovery times are a modelled baseline, not a live test, and the tabletop results are simulated.

Frameworks referenced: NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1, ISO 22301:2019 and its 2024 climate amendment, and the CISA #StopRansomware Guide.
